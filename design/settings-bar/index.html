Pdfs by Kash · Settings bar directions

Four ways to present where files go, what happens to the originals and quality, without the stock form-control look. Every frame is the real window at 720 pt. Each direction shows Keep, Replace, an interaction (popover or tooltip) and dark mode. Red dashed guides run through the Keep and Replace frames at the same x positions to show that nothing moves when you switch.

Nothing moves under the pointerEvery setting has a fixed slot. When a setting stops applying, its slot stays where it is, greys out and says why, the Geminio rule.
Say it in words, not widgetsValues read as plain language (“Next to originals”, “Replace”), so the bar describes what will happen.
Option cards over dropdownsSmall sets of choices get cards with a one-line explanation, as in Geminio’s settings, so the explanation doesn’t need a tooltip.
Family, not cloneLavender-tinted neutrals, hairline outlines, 8–10 pt radii and a soft accent tint for selection, the same as Geminio and Budget. Native SF type keeps it a Mac app.
A

Setting tiles

Three quiet tiles above the footer, each with an icon, a small caption and the current value in words. Clicking a tile opens a small popover of option cards. All three values stay visible at all times. In Replace, the destination tile keeps its place, greys out and says “In place”.

Tooltips: when, what, and how they look

Rules (adopted from Geminio’s FastTooltip)

  • Fewer tooltips. No tooltip on anything whose label already says it: Compress, Clear, Keep, Replace, quality names. The option cards carry the explanations instead.
  • Only for secondary detail: a truncated path (show the full path), icon-only buttons (“Show in Finder”, “Remove from list”), and a disabled control (say why).
  • Delay: about 0.7 s before the first tooltip, then instant while you move to neighbouring controls, the way macOS warms up. Upstream used the system default of about 1.5 s with heavy yellow boxes. The current build shows them almost at once.
  • Look: 11 pt, 6 pt radius, the app’s own floating surface with a hairline ring and a soft shadow. At most 280 pt wide, placed above the control so it never covers the next one, and it never takes clicks.
  • Never repeat the visible label.
